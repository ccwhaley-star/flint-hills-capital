Our Story

From One Small-Town Facility to a Midwest Portfolio

2020
Midwest Storage Solutions Founded

Chris founded Midwest Storage Solutions on a simple premise: the small-town facilities institutional buyers pass over don’t need an on-site manager. They need better systems.

2021
First Acquisitions

Acquired two Allen County facilities in Gas and Iola, Kansas — 124 units between them. The first real test of the model, and the start of the operating playbook.

2022
Townsend Storage

Acquired Townsend Storage in Parsons, Kansas — 125 units — and rebranded it as Labette County Storage. Began building the self-management operating model: reducing full-time staff hours, deploying AI-powered customer service, and automating the tenant lifecycle.

2023
Doubling Parsons

Doubled the Parsons site, adding non-climate-controlled and larger drive-up units to serve demand the original footprint couldn’t. The expansion carried the facility from 125 units to 244 and leased ahead of projections. Expense ratios settled into the 15–25% range — roughly half the industry norm. In September, acquired land in Fort Scott, Kansas, as a potential site for ground-up development.

2024
Butler County

Acquired and rebranded to Butler County Storage in El Dorado, Kansas — at 173 units, the largest single facility to that point — and sold the two original Allen County properties as a package that October. The model had completed a full turn: acquire, modernize, stabilize, and recycle capital into the next deal.

2025
Flint Hills Capital Formalized and Portfolio Scaled

Restructured the holding company as Flint Hills Capital, with Midwest Storage Solutions as the operating arm. In the same year, designed, permitted, and built Bourbon County Storage on the Fort Scott land acquired in 2023 — an 84-unit facility and the first ground-up development, which leased at nearly one unit per day after opening.

Closed on Reno County Storage in December — 477 units across Hutchinson and South Hutchinson, and the largest single transaction to date. The acquisition brought the owned portfolio to 805 units across four facilities.

2026
Scaling the Portfolio

Closed out Allen County in the spring with the sale of the climate-controlled facility — the last holding in the first market we entered — and added Sand Hills Storage in June under a triple net lease. The portfolio now stands at 1,109 units under management — 805 of them owned across four facilities — with expense ratios holding at 15–25% and four successful exits behind us. Full-time focus on acquisitions, ground-up development, and expansion into flex and small bay industrial space.

About

Founder & Operator

Chris Whaley
Founder & Managing Principal

Chris founded Midwest Storage Solutions in 2020 after 25 years in technology and private equity, and restructured the business as Flint Hills Capital in 2025. He identified an opportunity to apply the same data-driven, technology-first approach to an industry still largely run on paper and phone calls to create value.

He was raised in Lawrence, Kansas, and is a sixth-generation Kansan with over 30 years of experience in real estate and more than 50 successful transactions. Chris attended the University of Maryland, and is a US Marine veteran.
