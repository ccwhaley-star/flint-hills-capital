Acquisition Criteria

What We're Looking For

We acquire self storage and flex space facilities in Kansas and surrounding secondary markets. We also develop ground-up on sites where existing supply can't meet demand. If you own a facility and are considering a sale — or you're a broker with a listing that fits — we'd like to hear from you.

Contact Us

Market Profile

Secondary and tertiary markets in Kansas and adjacent states. Towns with 5,000–75,000 population, stable or growing demand.

Facility Size

50–400 units, single-site or small portfolios. Below the threshold institutional buyers will underwrite, above what a part-time owner can run well — the gap where our operating model creates the most value.

Seller Profile

Owners approaching retirement, families settling an estate, and operators whose facility has come to ask more time than they want to give it. What we buy is usually already profitable. We close quickly, often with SBA financing, and we stay flexible on structure.

Development Sites

4–10 acres with commercial zoning or rezoning potential in high-visibility locations near residential growth. We handle entitlements, design, and construction.
